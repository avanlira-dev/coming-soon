AVANLIRA FAQ

Questions before you start

What is Avanlira?

Avanlira is an AI marketing workspace for Indian small businesses. It brings content creation, campaign planning, scheduling, local SEO, competitor insights and analytics together in one place.

Who is Avanlira built for?

Avanlira is designed for small businesses, local shops, creators and lean marketing teams that want practical marketing help without needing a large agency or specialist team.

What can Avanlira create?

You can create social captions, hashtags, calls to action, campaign ideas, image concepts and short-form video concepts, then organise the work in your content library.

When is Avanlira launching?

How do AI content and image limits work?

Each plan includes a monthly allowance for AI content creations and image generations. Allowances reset each month according to your plan.
