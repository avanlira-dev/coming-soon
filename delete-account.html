Avanlira Account & Data Deletion

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

At Avanlira, we respect your data ownership and privacy. In compliance with Google Play Developer policies and global data privacy standards, you have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time.

Option 1: Immediate In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have access to the Avanlira mobile app or web platform, you can delete your account instantly:

  1. Open the Avanlira App and sign in to your account.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Account Management.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm your choice.

* In-app deletion executes immediately through our secure backend API, instantly removing your profile and active session.

Option 2: Submit External Deletion Request Form

If you no longer have the app installed or prefer to submit an external web deletion request, please complete the form below:

Must match the email address registered with your Avanlira account.

Request Received

Your account deletion request has been recorded. Our automated process queued your request for execution. Account deletion and data removal will be completed within 7 business days. A confirmation will be sent to your registered email address.

Option 3: Request via Direct Email

Alternatively, you can send an account deletion request directly to our customer support team:

To: support@avanlira.com

Subject: Avanlira Account Deletion Request

Body details to include:

  • Registered Email Address: your-email@example.com
  • Explicit confirmation statement: "Please permanently delete my Avanlira account and associated data."

Data Deletion Details & Policy

Permanently Deleted Data

  • Account profile details (Name, Email, Hashed Auth credentials).
  • Connected Meta (Facebook Pages / Instagram Business) access tokens and account mappings.
  • Saved brand assets, logo uploads, and business configurations.
  • Generated AI captions, image history, post drafts, and scheduled social media posts.

Data Retention Exceptions

  • Billing transaction receipts (retained as required by law for tax/accounting purposes).
  • Aggregated, anonymized telemetry metrics (containing no personal identifiers).

Execution Timeline: In-app deletions execute instantly. External requests submitted via the form above or email are processed within 7 business days.

Revoking Social Platform Access

If you wish to remove Avanlira's access to your Facebook Page or Instagram Business account without deleting your entire Avanlira account, you can disconnect your social accounts directly in the Avanlira mobile app or revoke permissions via Meta Business Integrations Settings.
