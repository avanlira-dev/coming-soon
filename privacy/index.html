Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Welcome to Avanlira ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a transparent, secure experience when using our marketing automation, social media scheduling, and content tools.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, connecting third-party platforms, or generating marketing material:

  • Account Details: Your name, email address, password, and business brand profile.
  • Connected Social Media Accounts (Meta / Facebook & Instagram): When you connect your Facebook Pages or Instagram Business accounts via Meta OAuth, we receive access tokens, Page IDs, account names, and profile pictures authorized by you.
  • User Content: Text prompts, post drafts, scheduled posts, images, and brand assets saved in your Avanlira workspace.
  • Technical Data: Basic usage statistics, sign-in IP addresses, browser info, and application logs to ensure system reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to provide, maintain, and improve our services:

  • To publish and schedule content to your Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts as explicitly instructed by you.
  • To store and refresh long-lived access tokens required to keep your authorized social media channels connected.
  • To generate AI content, captions, and graphics tailored to your business profile.
  • To notify you about account updates, subscription alerts, and customer support inquiries.

3. Meta API Data Usage & Security

Avanlira accesses Meta Graph API data strictly in accordance with Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies:

  • No Selling of Data: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data or Meta account tokens to third parties.
  • Token Storage: Access tokens are stored securely in encrypted databases with strict Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Revocation & Disconnection: You can disconnect your Facebook or Instagram account at any time directly from the Avanlira Connections dashboard or by revoking access in your Meta User Settings. Upon disconnection, stored access tokens are deleted.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with reliable infrastructure providers to deliver our service:

  • Supabase: For secure authentication and database hosting.
  • Meta Graph API: For publishing content to Facebook and Instagram.
  • Stripe: For secure subscription processing (we do not store credit card details).

5. Data Deletion Requests

You have full control over your data. If you wish to delete your account, remove connected social accounts, or request complete data deletion, you can do so by:

  • Clicking Disconnect Account under the Social Connections page in the application.
  • Sending a data deletion request to support@avanlira.com. We process all deletion requests within 30 days.
